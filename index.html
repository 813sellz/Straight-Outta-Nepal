Team Profile

Straight Outta Nepal has grown from a community-driven roster into a recognizable PUBG Mobile brand with qualifier success, public tournament results, and a standout visual identity.

  • North America-based PUBG Mobile organization
  • Founded in 2020
  • Competes under the SON tag
  • Owner: Arman Bhandari, Columbia, South Carolina
